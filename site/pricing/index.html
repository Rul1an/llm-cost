Architecture

01

Fetch Manifest

CLI requests signed manifest.json from Worker. Verifies basic integrity.

02

Verify Signature

Ed25519 check against embedded public key. Anti-replay timestamp check.

03

Stream & Verify

Downloads DB artifact. Validates SHA256 matches manifest before atomic write.

FAQ

Why is there a rate limit on the free tier?
To prevent abuse of our bandwidth. The 10/day limit is sufficient for most individual developers and small CI pipelines. Pro removes this limit for heavy usage.
Can I self-host this?
Yes. The Enterprise plan provides a Docker container for the Pricing Service and tools to sign your own custom pricing databases ("Private Root of Trust").
What if I lose my license key?
You can recover it via the Recovery Page using your purchase email, or check your original receipt email.
